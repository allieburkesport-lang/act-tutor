How the plan works
29 concepts are on the SAT (19 math, 10 English). Each learning day teaches 3 new concepts: 2 math + 1 English (math first). Every day is about 65% math / 35% English, because the Naval Academy weighs math more. For each one: open the lesson, then do the 10-question drill.
Then the review drill: 1 question from every concept already covered, plus extra questions for his weakest concepts (math first, to keep the day at 65% math). It grows each day, so nothing gets forgotten.
Every night at midnight the plan checks yesterday's answers. Concepts he didn't practise move to the next day, review shifts to his weakest concepts, and Dec 2–3 fill with his weakest ones.
