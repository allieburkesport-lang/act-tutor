How the plan works
29 concepts are on the SAT (19 math, 10 English). Each learning day teaches 3 new concepts: 2 math + 1 English (math first). For each one: open the lesson, then do the 10-question drill.
Then the review drill: 1 question from every concept already covered, and a 2nd one for each concept he's under 60% on. It grows each day, so nothing gets forgotten.
Every night at midnight the plan checks yesterday's answers. Concepts he didn't practise move to the next day, review shifts to his weakest concepts, and Dec 2–3 fill with his weakest ones.
