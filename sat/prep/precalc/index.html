HOW THIS CLASS WORKS

The rules that decide the grade
Unlock orderSyllabus Quiz at 100% → ALEKS Initial Knowledge Check → learn 70% of a unit’s topics to unlock that Unit Quiz → Honorlock Practice Quiz unlocks both exams.
Placement test: no guessingThe Initial Knowledge Check removes every topic it thinks you know. A lucky guess means that topic shows up on a proctored exam you never studied. Do it alone and say “I don’t know” when you don’t.
Unit quizzes: free pointsUnlimited attempts, and a retake only re-asks the ones you missed. Keep going until each one is at 100%. Together they are 40% of the grade.
Exams: only 2 triesMidterm (20%) and Final (40%) are proctored on Honorlock with 2 attempts each, and the higher score counts. Do the practice quizzes first; they have the same number of questions.
CalculatorDesmos is allowed on exams. Anything with CAS is not. Learn Desmos now so it is fast on test day.
No AI on problemsThe syllabus says not to use AI on coursework. Use the Math Support Team for help on ALEKS problems.
