🔀 How the Two Tracks Work in Parallel

Think of it as two separate applications happening at the same time. Neither depends on the other to move forward.

Track A
ROTC National Scholarship
Apply directly to the military
1 Submit application at netfocus.navy.mil/nrotc (NROTC) or goarmy.com/rotc (AROTC)
2 Pass fitness assessment (CFA for Navy, ACFT for Army) + DoDMERB medical
3 Scholarship board reviews GPA, test scores, fitness, essays, interview
4 If awarded: Select a host school from the approved list. The scholarship follows you there.
? If not awarded: Can still join as a College Programmer (no scholarship, but still commissioning path)
Track B
College Admissions
Apply through Common App separately
1 Apply to target schools via Common App (NROTC host schools listed below)
2 Schools make admission decisions independently — they don't require ROTC scholarship
3 If accepted to a school with an NROTC unit, Patrick can enroll in ROTC even without the national scholarship
✓ Best case: Win national scholarship AND get accepted to a host school — scholarship pays tuition, room & board, stipend
Bottom line for Patrick: He should submit the NROTC scholarship application (due Jan 31, 2027) at the same time he's applying to colleges via Common App. No acceptance letter required — the scholarship is judged entirely on his own merit. Being accepted to a school doesn't guarantee the scholarship, and having the scholarship doesn't guarantee admission. They are independent.

🏥 DoDMERB Medical — One Exam, Used Everywhere

DoDMERB (Department of Defense Medical Examination Review Board) is the standard military medical clearance exam. The same exam results are accepted by USNA, NROTC, and AROTC — you do not need three separate physicals. Once scheduled and completed (usually through a local examiner), the results are shared to all military programs you're applying to.

Schedule when: As soon as you receive a candidate questionnaire or are otherwise eligible (typically summer/fall 2026).
Common disqualifiers: Asthma, color vision deficiencies, certain surgeries, allergies — many are waiverable. If flagged, apply for a waiver immediately rather than giving up.
Contact for NROTC: Your NROTC scholarship application will trigger the DoDMERB invitation once your academic record is reviewed.
